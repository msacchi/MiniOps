All operators support the standard MiniOps forward/adjoint syntax:

y = A * x
xadj = A' * y

Sampling convention

sampling_op(idx, n) samples scalar entries of a vector. For arrays with time in dimension 1, trace_sampling_op(idx, size(X)) samples complete traces and returns an nt x length(idx) matrix.
